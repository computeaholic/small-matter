Soundscape Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. Publisher and privacy contact: Jeffrey Smith, sendtojeffsmith@gmail.com.

Soundscape Studio is a listening and mixing app. You do not need an app account. The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK, app-operated cloud service, or automatic upload of your mixes or listening activity to Jeffrey Smith. Audio is generated or played from ingredients bundled with the app.

Information on your device

The app stores saved mixes, ingredient settings, favorites, the last selected scene, playback preferences and purchase-access records locally. Purchase-access records can include product and transaction identifiers, verification dates, expiration or revocation information, and limited offline-access state. These records help the app decide which features are available; they do not create a purchase.

The app uses Apple's audio and media controls. A scene or personal mix name can appear in system Now Playing controls, including the Lock Screen. iOS may keep diagnostic information under your device settings. The app does not record your microphone, request your contacts or location, or contain an app-operated remote audio service.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles purchase, payment, verification, restoration and subscription-management services. The app receives the product and transaction information it needs to provide access. Jeffrey Smith does not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card details through the app. Apple's services and privacy choices are described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing a mix

Export is a choice you make. It opens the system share sheet for a JSON copy of the current editing mix; it does not export audio or back up your entire Library. Mix names and ingredient settings are included. The destination you choose controls its copy. Import reads a recipe file you select and adds a validated mix with a new identity. The app does not automatically send imported or exported recipes to the developer.

Beta feedback and support

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with Jeffrey Smith. You cannot opt out of this collection while using TestFlight. For email-invited testing, Apple also provides tester identity information, including your name and email address. Reports include device/build details. If you submit feedback or screenshots, Apple shares those submissions with Jeffrey Smith to investigate issues and improve the app. You can stop participating through TestFlight; that does not remove records already held by a provider. This is separate from the app's lack of an added analytics SDK. See TestFlight & Privacy.

If you email support, your email provider and the developer's email provider process your address, message and any attachments. Only send information you want to share. Check screenshots and recipe files for private details first; passwords, passcodes and payment details are not needed for support. You can use the app without emailing feedback.

Support messages and beta reports are used to respond to requests, investigate defects, improve Soundscape Studio and meet applicable obligations. They are not sold or used for advertising. TestFlight reports are not shared with third parties. Support messages and beta reports are retained only while needed for those purposes or applicable recordkeeping requirements. Provider-managed records, backups and Apple-held information are subject to those providers' controls and retention.

Deletion, recovery and your choices

Deleting a mix removes it from the active Library after the change is saved. The app's rolling recovery copy can retain the preceding Library until a later save; session Undo can also retain an earlier copy while the app is open. Deletion is not a secure-erasure operation. Exported files and device/cloud backups are managed separately by you and their providers. Restore Purchases restores purchase access, not mixes.

Export important mixes before removing the app or losing access to an expiring beta. There is no remote Soundscape Studio account or developer-held cloud Library to delete. Removing the app is subject to iOS rules and does not remove copies you exported or that are held in backups.

For questions or requests about information you sent to Jeffrey Smith, including access, correction or deletion, email sendtojeffsmith@gmail.com. Requests are handled subject to applicable rights and obligations, reasonable identity checks and the information actually held. Contact Apple or another provider for information held independently by that provider. Withdrawing optional feedback does not remove access to the app's listening features.

Policy and support webpages

These pages contain no advertising, analytics code, embedded forms or third-party fonts, and set no tracking cookies themselves. A hosting provider still processes connection information needed to serve webpages, such as an IP address and request details. If served through GitHub Pages, that service is covered by GitHub's Privacy Statement. Opening an external link or sending email uses the chosen service under its own policies.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a revised date. Material changes will be disclosed as appropriate before the changed practice is used. Questions can be sent to the privacy contact above.
