Soundscape Studio Support

Soundscape Studio is published by Jeffrey Smith. Email sendtojeffsmith@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. Beta testers can also use TestFlight → Send Beta Feedback.

Start here

  • Choose an environment in Listen and tap Play. Use the player to pause or return to what is playing.
  • Personalize balances ingredients. Solo lets you hear one ingredient; occasional sounds get a short preview. Mute can be saved with your mix. Playback level is shared across scenes.
  • Save a mix to Library to return to it. Old saved mixes can keep an earlier ingredient version; choose the current factory scene to evaluate a new sound.
  • Optional binaural tones need stereo headphones for binaural perception. They are not a treatment.

Before troubleshooting

Keep the app installed. Open each important saved mix in Personalize, confirm its name, finish editing, then open Settings → Export Current Recipe. Save the JSON somewhere you control. Repeat for other mixes. Purchases and Restore do not back up or restore your Library.

If sound is missing, check Play/Pause, the system's current output route and media volume, the app's playback level, and ingredient Mute/Solo settings. Choose a comfortable level rather than turning it up sharply. If purchase options are unavailable, use Try Again or continue with Free scenes and report it. Use Restore Purchases for eligible existing purchases; do not make repeated purchases to clear an error.

Report a problem

Please include the app version/build from Settings, iPhone model, iOS version, scene or mix, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Mention the output device if relevant and whether it repeats. Screenshots or TestFlight feedback can help. Review attachments first and omit private mix names or recipe files unless they are needed. Never send passwords, passcodes or payment details.

Beta and purchases

The initial invitation-only beta is intended for the agreed iPhones. iPad testing is a later phase. TestFlight builds and access can expire; export important mixes before leaving. Follow the sandbox Premium purchase/Restore instructions supplied with your invitation. A TestFlight invitation alone does not unlock Premium, and a Debug development evaluation label is not a real purchase.

Apple handles billing, subscriptions and refunds. Buying Premium Lifetime does not cancel a separate subscription. Manage subscriptions in Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions or through the app's Manage Subscriptions action.
