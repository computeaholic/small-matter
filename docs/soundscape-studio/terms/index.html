Listening features

Somewhere Yours provides environmental and intentionally synthetic audio. Free includes nine environments, core ingredient mixing, basic binaural controls, timers, background playback, local saves, favorites, and recipe import/export. Premium adds ten environments and advanced spatial/composition controls. The app shows which features require access.

Optional binaural tones are listening ingredients. Somewhere Yours makes no medical, therapeutic, cognitive or other health-outcome promise. Use a comfortable volume. Lower it or stop if listening feels uncomfortable, and use audio in a way that lets you remain aware of your surroundings when needed.

Your mixes

Mixes are stored on your device. Save changes you want to keep and export important mixes as JSON to a location you control. Export shares the current editing mix, not the whole Library or an audio file. Playback level is a shared listening adjustment and is not saved as a recipe ingredient.

Loss of Premium access does not delete saved mixes. Premium personal compositions already saved on this device remain playable and editable with core mixing and tone controls after subscription expiry under the established saved-recipe admission rules. You can save, duplicate and export that work. Its existing advanced settings stay saved and continue to play; new advanced edits, new Premium factory scenes and Premium imports require current Premium access. A recipe file does not create or restore purchase entitlement. Importing a Premium recipe requires access even on the same device.

This protects existing local work rather than providing cloud synchronization. Continued use depends on keeping the mix files and a usable app installation; it does not guarantee indefinite beta access.

The app maintains a rolling recovery copy, but this is not a cloud backup or a guarantee against data loss. Deleting a mix, removing the app, changing devices or losing beta access can affect availability. Previously exported copies and device backups are managed separately. Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step; contact support and export important mixes first.
