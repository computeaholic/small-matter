Listening features

Soundscape Studio provides environmental and intentionally synthetic audio. Free includes six environments, core ingredient mixing, basic binaural controls, timers, background playback, local saves, favorites, and recipe import/export. Premium adds six environments and advanced spatial/composition controls. The app shows which features require access.

Optional binaural tones are listening ingredients. Soundscape Studio makes no medical, therapeutic, cognitive or other health-outcome promise. Use a comfortable volume. Lower it or stop if listening feels uncomfortable, and use audio in a way that lets you remain aware of your surroundings when needed.

Premium purchases

Apple processes purchases. Available products, the full price, billing period and currency are shown using current StoreKit information before purchase; no price stated elsewhere overrides that information. Monthly and annual subscriptions provide Premium while the subscription entitlement is valid. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple in accordance with the purchase terms.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in the app's Manage Subscriptions action or Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds and purchase disputes are handled through Apple's purchase-support process and applicable consumer rights.

Premium Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable Premium entitlement with no scheduled subscription expiry. Access remains subject to Apple's verification, refund/revocation rules and app availability. It is not a subscription or a guarantee about a different future product. Buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription; cancel any unwanted subscription separately through Apple.

Restore Purchases asks Apple to restore eligible purchase access for the Apple Account in use. It does not restore mix files. Internet access can be needed to purchase, restore or refresh verification. Previously verified access has a bounded offline policy; indefinite offline Premium access is not promised. No external payment, account password or payment-card information is requested by the developer.

Your mixes

Mixes are stored on your device. Save changes you want to keep and export important mixes as JSON to a location you control. Export shares the current editing mix, not the whole Library or an audio file. Playback level is a shared listening adjustment and is not saved as a recipe ingredient.

Loss of Premium access does not delete saved mixes. Core use and export of retained personal mixes remain available under the app's access rules; new Premium factory scenes, Premium imports and advanced editing require access. A recipe file is not proof of a purchase and cannot grant an entitlement.

The app maintains a rolling recovery copy, but this is not a cloud backup or a guarantee against data loss. Deleting a mix, removing the app, changing devices or losing beta access can affect availability. Previously exported copies and device backups are managed separately. Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step; contact support and export important mixes first.
